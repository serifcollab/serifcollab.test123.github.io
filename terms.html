Service description

LifeSync is a personal SMS assistant that sends automated text messages including reminders, calendar summaries, and event notifications. This service is operated by and for the sole use of the account holder.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on the user's calendar, email activity, and manual requests. Typical usage is 5–20 messages per day.

Message and data rates

Standard message and data rates from your wireless carrier may apply.

Opt-out

You can stop receiving messages at any time by texting STOP to the LifeSync number. You will receive a confirmation message and no further texts will be sent.

Help

Text HELP to the LifeSync number for assistance, or contact the account holder directly.

LifeSync — Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 4, 2026

What is LifeSync?

LifeSync is a personal-use SMS assistant that sends automated reminders, calendar notifications, and daily briefings to the account holder via text message.

Who uses this service?

This service is used exclusively by the account holder who set it up. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from third parties.

What data is processed?

LifeSync processes the following data solely for the account holder:

  • Calendar events from Google Calendar
  • Email metadata and summaries from Gmail
  • Incoming SMS content (forwarded by the user's own device)
  • Conversation history between the user and the assistant

How is data stored?

Data is stored in a private Google Sheet owned by the account holder. Conversation history and event logs are retained for context and can be deleted at any time by the account holder.

Third-party services

LifeSync uses the following third-party services to function:

  • Twilio — for sending and receiving SMS messages
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — for AI-powered message analysis
  • n8n — for workflow automation
  • Google APIs — for calendar and email access

Each service is governed by its own privacy policy. No data is sold or shared for advertising purposes.

Opting out

The account holder can stop all messages at any time by texting STOP to the LifeSync number or by deactivating the workflows.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the account holder directly.
